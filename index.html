⚠ SITUATIONAL AWARENESS ONLY

This application is a personal training and situational-awareness aid. It is not a certified navigation source and must not be used for primary navigation. Aerodrome data, frequencies, and airspace shown here are approximate, unverified placeholders — verify everything against the current CFS, VNC/VTA, and NOTAMs. Airspace is drawn as simplified circles and does not represent real boundaries. Positions rely on device GPS with no integrity monitoring.
